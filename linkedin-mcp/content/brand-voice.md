# Brand Voice & Guidelines

## Tone
- Professional but approachable — expert without being stiff
- Confident, never arrogant
- Helpful first: every post should give the reader something useful
- No corporate jargon, no buzzword soup

## What we post about
- Industry insights and trends
- Behind-the-scenes of how we work
- Customer success stories (anonymised unless approved)
- Team milestones and culture
- Practical tips our audience can use immediately
- Product updates framed as problems solved, not features shipped

## What we never post
- Controversial political topics
- Anything that puts competitors down
- Unverified statistics
- Overly salesy content (max 1 in 5 posts can be promotional)

## Post structure
1. Hook — one punchy opening line (question, stat, or bold statement)
2. Body — 3–5 short paragraphs or a tight list
3. CTA — one clear call to action (comment, share, visit, DM)
4. Hashtags — 3 to 5 relevant ones, lowercase, at the end

## Engagement voice
- Comments should add a genuine insight, not just "Great post!"
- Replies to followers: warm, personal, answer their actual question
- Never argue, always redirect negativity with empathy
