# Weekly Post Calendar

One post per weekday. Rotate through pillars so the feed stays varied.

| Day       | Pillar              | Format suggestion          |
|-----------|---------------------|----------------------------|
| Monday    | Industry Education  | Numbered list or how-to    |
| Tuesday   | Company Story       | Short narrative paragraph  |
| Wednesday | Product Value       | Before/after or stat-led   |
| Thursday  | Community & Trends  | React to a recent headline |
| Friday    | Social Proof        | Quote or milestone         |
