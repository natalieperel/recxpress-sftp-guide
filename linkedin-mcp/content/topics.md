# Content Pillars & Topics

## Pillar 1 — Industry Education
Teach the audience something they didn't know.
Examples: how SFTP security works, common data transfer mistakes, compliance tips

## Pillar 2 — Company Story
Show who we are and how we think.
Examples: how we solved a hard customer problem, what our onboarding process looks like, team wins

## Pillar 3 — Product Value
Demonstrate real-world impact without sounding like an ad.
Examples: before/after workflow comparison, time saved, errors eliminated

## Pillar 4 — Community & Trends
Stay relevant by reacting to industry news.
Examples: new compliance regulations, security breaches in our space, tooling trends

## Pillar 5 — Social Proof
Let results speak.
Examples: customer quotes (anonymised), case study snippets, usage milestones
